# Beta User Guide

## What Market Room is

Market Room is a beta market-intelligence website with specialist agents for Macro, Rates, Commodities, FX, Risk/Sentiment, and Equities.

It is designed to answer market questions with mechanism-based reasoning: what changed, why it matters, what signal may be false, and what to watch next.

## Best way to use it

Ask specific market questions.

Strong prompts:

- “NVDA is down after earnings despite a revenue beat. Is this margin pressure, guidance quality, real-yield pressure, or multiple compression?”
- “WTI inventories drew 6 million barrels and Cushing is below 25 million barrels. Is this real physical tightness?”
- “Core CPI is hot but shelter looks lagged. Should Macro treat this as OER distortion or active demand-pull inflation?”
- “EUR/USD is falling while rate differentials move against Europe. Is this central-bank divergence or dollar liquidity stress?”
- “AI winners are crowded and correlations are rising. Is this a degrossing setup?”

Weak prompts:

- “What should I buy?”
- “Tell me the best stock.”
- “What will the market do tomorrow?”

## What to look for in a good answer

A strong answer should:

- name the relevant specialist lens
- identify the mechanism
- separate signal from noise
- mention thresholds or confirmation points where relevant
- explain what would invalidate the view
- avoid pretending to know what is unknowable

## What feedback is most useful

Please report:

- wrong agent answered
- answer was too generic
- answer missed an obvious instrument or stock
- answer ignored your follow-up context
- answer gave a list when you wanted attribution
- answer gave attribution when you wanted names

This beta is meant to improve through real misses.
