## Rates Knowledge Workspace

Use this folder for curated long-term research that helps the Rates Agent reason through Treasury yields, curve moves, duration pressure, inflation expectations, and policy repricing.

Suggested structure:

- `foundations/`
- `frameworks/`
- `event-playbooks/`
- `house-view-notes/`

Good examples:

- curve-steepener / flattener frameworks
- duration shock post-mortems
- term-premium notes
- inflation expectation playbooks
- Fed repricing and Treasury-market stress notes

Best practice:

- store concise interpretations of past rate episodes
- summarize research into signals, triggers, and scenario notes
- avoid raw full-history bond datasets in the vector store

Upload these files from the Rates Agent section on the Admin page.
