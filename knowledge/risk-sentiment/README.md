## Risk-Sentiment Knowledge Workspace

Use this folder for curated long-term research that helps the Risk/Sentiment Agent reason through positioning, crowding, volatility regimes, and risk-on versus risk-off behavior.

Suggested structure:

- `foundations/`
- `frameworks/`
- `event-playbooks/`
- `house-view-notes/`

Good examples:

- crowding and positioning checklists
- vol regime summaries
- stress / de-risking playbooks
- breadth-fragility notes
- market psychology and sentiment post-mortems

Best practice:

- use concise notes and post-mortems that teach reusable judgment
- summarize large market commentaries into frameworks and trigger lists
- avoid noisy raw social-firehose or giant unfiltered news dumps

Upload these files from the Risk/Sentiment Agent section on the Admin page.
