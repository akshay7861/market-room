## Valuation Framework — Where Multiples Break Down

P/E multiples are a function of earnings growth AND the discount rate. Both must be tracked simultaneously:

**Forward P/E sustainable ranges by rate regime:**

| 10Y Real Yield | Sustainable Forward P/E | Historical precedent |
|---------------|------------------------|---------------------|
| Negative | 22–28x | 2017–2021 bull market |
| 0%–1.0% | 18–22x | Pre-COVID normal |
| 1.0%–2.0% | 15–19x | 2018 tightening, early 2022 |
| Above 2.0% | 13–16x | 2022 bear market lows |

**Current**: 10Y real yield at 1.89%, S&P forward P/E near 20x. At 1.89% real yields, 20x is slightly above the middle of the 15–19x sustainable range. The multiple is not cheap, but not crisis-level extended. The risk is if real yields rise above 2% — that historically triggers the 16–17x zone.

**Key rule**: when valuation is in the upper half of the sustainable range AND earnings growth is decelerating, that is the highest-risk combination. Earnings must grow INTO the multiple or the multiple must compress. There is no third option.

## Earnings Quality Checklist

Not all earnings beats are equal. Run this check before calling a beat bullish:

**Durable beat (bullish signal):**
- Revenue beat + margin expansion + forward guidance raised
- EPS beat driven by volume/pricing, not cost cuts alone
- Free cash flow growth exceeds reported EPS growth

**Hollow beat (fade the pop):**
- EPS beat driven by buybacks or tax rate changes (not operations)
- Revenue miss with margin expansion (cost-cutting, not growth)
- Forward guidance cut or withdrawn
- "Beat" because consensus estimates were sandbagged by management

**Miss with guidance:**
- Revenue miss + guidance cut = multiple contraction ahead
- EPS miss with revenue beat + margin disappointment = operating leverage broke down

**Rule**: in a late-cycle environment, a company beating on EPS but cutting revenue guidance is a sell signal, not a buy signal. The market has historically punished this combination by 5–8% on results day.

## Breadth Framework — Confirm the Index Move

Index-level moves lie. Breadth tells the truth.

**Healthy bull market breadth:**
- >60% of S&P 500 components above their 50-day moving average
- New 52-week highs >100 stocks/week (out of 500)
- IWM (small caps) outperforming or matching SPY performance
- Equal-weight S&P outperforming market-cap-weighted S&P

**Distribution phase (warning):**
- S&P index at or near highs BUT <40% of components above 50-day MA
- New 52-week highs declining while index rises
- IWM underperforming SPY by >5% over 3 months (small caps pricing tighter financial conditions)
- Mega-cap tech carrying the index while equal-weight S&P lags

**The trap**: SPY can appear to be in a bull market while 60% of stocks are already in a bear market. Always check IWM vs SPY and equal-weight vs market-cap weighted.

**Current IWM context**: IWM at ~269 vs SPY at ~700. IWM/SPY ratio at historic low relative to long-run averages. Small caps have been underperforming for 18+ months. This is a breadth concern, not a confirmation of bull market health.

## Sector Rotation Playbook

Sector leadership tells you where the market is in the cycle:

**Early cycle (after recession trough):**
- Leaders: Consumer Discretionary (XLY), Financials (XLF), Industrials
- Laggards: Utilities, Consumer Staples
- Signal: economically sensitive sectors outperform as growth recovers

**Mid cycle (expansion):**
- Leaders: Technology (XLK), Healthcare
- Laggards: Materials, Energy
- Signal: secular growth leaders extend; cyclicals fade

**Late cycle (before recession):**
- Leaders: Energy (XLE), Materials (XLB), Healthcare
- Laggards: Financials (NIM compression), Discretionary
- Signal: inflation beneficiaries and defensives outperform

**Recession:**
- Leaders: Utilities, Consumer Staples, Healthcare
- Laggards: Financials, Industrials, Discretionary
- Signal: pure defensive rotation

**XLF signal**: Financials (XLF) outperforming the S&P going into a rate-cutting cycle is a bull signal (market believes cuts will be soft-landing cuts, not recession cuts). Financials underperforming as rates fall = market is worried about recession and NIM compression — BEARISH.

## Earnings Cycle and Estimate Revision Dynamics

**Estimate revision momentum:**
- When >60% of S&P companies are seeing upward EPS revisions = earnings revision breadth positive → bullish
- When <40% seeing upward revisions = earnings revision breadth negative → caution
- Estimate revisions typically lead price by 3–6 weeks

**EPS growth vs revenue growth divergence:**
- EPS growing faster than revenue = margin expansion (good in early-to-mid cycle, unsustainable late cycle)
- Revenue growing faster than EPS = investment mode or cost pressure (watch which)
- Both declining = late cycle or recession

**Margin cycle:**
- Gross margins typically peak 3–6 months before the cycle peak as input costs rise
- Operating margins peak later (1–2 quarters after gross margins)
- Net margins peak last due to financial leverage
- Watch gross margin direction first — it's the leading indicator

## Volatility and Risk Appetite Gate

Use VIX as a regime gate, not a trading signal:

- **VIX below 15**: maximum complacency. Equities priced for perfection. Any negative surprise will have outsized downward impact.
- **VIX 15–20**: normal market. Moderate risk pricing. Dips are buyable.
- **VIX 20–30**: elevated risk. Market expecting further volatility. Reduce size on new longs.
- **VIX above 30**: fear/stress. Start considering whether it's a washout opportunity or a regime shift. Check if HY spreads are >500bps (if yes, regime shift; if no, potentially a washout buy).
- **VIX above 40**: capitulation zone. Historically marks the panic low with 3–6 month forward returns highly positive. 2008: peaked at 80. 2020: peaked at 85. 2022: peaked at 36.

**Current VIX ~18**: normal range. Not signaling either complacency or stress. Dips should be evaluated on fundamentals, not reflexively bought or sold.

## Cross-Asset Confirmation

An equity view is more credible when confirmed by other markets:

**Bullish equity — confirm with:**
- HY spreads stable or tightening (credit agreeing with equity optimism)
- Copper and industrial metals rising (demand-driven growth signal)
- IWM outperforming (small caps confirming breadth)
- VIX falling into the move

**Bearish equity — confirm with:**
- HY spreads widening (credit leading equities lower)
- Defensive sectors outperforming (staples, utilities, healthcare)
- IWM underperforming sharply (small cap stress ahead of large cap)
- Dollar strengthening aggressively (global risk-off)

## Failure Modes to Avoid

**1. Extrapolating mega-cap tech strength to the entire market.** SPY is market-cap weighted. The top 10 holdings (Mag-7 + 3) represent ~35% of the index. SPY can make new highs while 60% of the market is in a bear market. Always verify with equal-weight S&P and IWM before calling a bull market.

**2. Buying "beats" when guidance is being cut.** EPS beats driven by cost cuts and buybacks with declining revenue guidance are NOT bull signals. Companies managing the income statement while the business deteriorates. In 2022–2023, this pattern reliably preceded further multiple compression.
