## Equities Knowledge Workspace

Use this folder for curated long-term research that helps the Equities Agent reason through earnings cycles, sector rotation, leadership, breadth, and valuation pressure.

Suggested structure:

- `foundations/`
- `frameworks/`
- `event-playbooks/`
- `house-view-notes/`

Good examples:

- market-breadth and leadership frameworks
- earnings revision playbooks
- sector rotation notes
- valuation compression case studies
- regime notes on risk-on versus defensive leadership

Best practice:

- upload concise research notes and post-mortems
- summarize large brokerage reports into reusable frameworks
- avoid raw bulk price-history exports

Upload these files from the Equities Agent section on the Admin page.
