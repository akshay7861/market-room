# LinkedIn Beta Launch Post

I have been building a small market-intelligence platform called Market Room, and I am opening it up for beta testing:

https://market-room-web.pages.dev

The idea is simple:

Most market tools either give you raw data, generic summaries, or a single chatbot answer. I wanted to build something closer to a live investment discussion room, where different specialist agents look at the same market from different angles.

Market Room currently has six specialist agents:

- Macro
- Rates
- Commodities
- FX
- Risk/Sentiment
- Equities

They are not just summarising news. Each agent has its own internal playbooks, frameworks, instrument guides, false-signal checks, and rolling memory. The system routes questions to the right specialist, lets agents build on prior theses, and tries to explain market moves through mechanisms rather than generic commentary.

Examples of what you can ask:

- Why is a stock down despite a revenue beat?
- Is an oil inventory draw real physical tightness or just refinery noise?
- Is a bond move about Fed repricing, term premium, or inflation expectations?
- Which clean-energy stocks should I watch if grid investment accelerates?
- Is this a risk-on rally or crowded positioning unwind?

This is still beta. It will make mistakes. That is exactly why I want people to test it with real market questions and tell me where the reasoning breaks.

The goal is not to replace an analyst or a terminal. The goal is to build a more useful layer between raw market information and investment reasoning.

If you try it, I would love feedback on:

- which answers felt useful
- which answers felt generic
- where the wrong agent answered
- where the reasoning missed the market mechanism
- what you would want it to remember next time
