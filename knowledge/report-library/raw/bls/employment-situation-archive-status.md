 Archived News Releases | The Employment Situation

 The Employment Situation news release presents data from the Current Employment Statistics and Current Population Survey programs.

 
 Caution

 Data in archived news releases may have been revised in subsequent releases. The latest data, including any revisions, may be obtained from the databases accessible on the program homepages:

 
 Current Employment Statistics: databases 

 Current Population Survey: databases
