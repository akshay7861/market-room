 Archived News Releases | Consumer Price Index

 The Consumer Price Index (CPI) news release presents data from the Consumer Price Indexes program.

 
 Caution

 Data in archived news releases may have been revised in subsequent releases. The latest data, including any revisions, may be obtained from the databases accessible on the program homepage: Consumer Price Indexes: databases .
