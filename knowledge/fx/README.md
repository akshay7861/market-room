## FX Knowledge Workspace

Use this folder for curated long-term research that helps the FX Agent reason through dollar cycles, major crosses, policy divergence, carry, and cross-border transmission.

Suggested structure:

- `foundations/`
- `frameworks/`
- `event-playbooks/`
- `house-view-notes/`

Good examples:

- dollar smile notes
- carry and funding stress frameworks
- central-bank divergence playbooks
- EM FX contagion post-mortems
- trade and capital-flow transmission notes

Best practice:

- turn large macro/FX reports into concise retrieval-friendly summaries
- store scenario trees and trigger checklists
- avoid giant raw tick data or uncurated scraped article dumps

Upload these files from the FX Agent section on the Admin page.
