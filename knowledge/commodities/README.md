## Commodities Knowledge Workspace

Use this folder to keep the curated research files you want the Commodities Agent to retrieve from as long-term sector memory.

Suggested structure:

- `foundations/`
- `frameworks/`
- `event-playbooks/`
- `house-view-notes/`

Keep files short, opinionated, and readable. Good examples:

- a one-page oil market framework
- a note on natural gas seasonality
- a copper demand checklist
- a gold and real-yields explainer
- a house-view memo on commodity inflation risk

Avoid huge raw datasets. This vector store works best with curated text notes in `.md`, `.txt`, `.pdf`, or `.docx`.

This is retrieval memory, not model training. For future autonomy, distill historical data and external reports into reusable notes, playbooks, and house-view summaries.

Once the app is running, upload these files from the Commodities Agent section on the Admin page.
